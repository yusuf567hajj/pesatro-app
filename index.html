Step-by-Step Daraja Setup Guide

Step 1: Create a Developer Account

Go to the Safaricom Developer Portal and click Sign Up. Enter your details to create your developer account.

Step 2: Create a New App

Navigate to My Apps and click Create New App. Enable Lipa Na M-PESA Online API (STK Push) for your Paybill or Till Number.

Step 3: Copy Your Keys & Configure Callback URL

Copy your Consumer Key, Consumer Secret, and Passkey. Set your Callback URL to your domain endpoint (e.g. https://pesatro-app.vercel.app/api/callback).

Step 4: Save & Lock in Creator Studio

Paste all values into your Pesatro Creator Studio. Click Save & Lock Credentials. Your browser will store them permanently so you never have to re-enter them.
